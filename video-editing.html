FAQ

Questions about video editing.

Do you shoot the videos too?
Our main work is editing. You can record on your phone and we will guide you on what to film so the edit comes out well.
Can captions be in Urdu?
Yes. Captions can be in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, whichever suits your audience.
Can you post the videos for us?
Yes. If you also take our social media management, we schedule and post them for you.
How long does one edit take?
Short reels are usually ready within a couple of days of getting your clips. Longer videos or big batches take more time, and we tell you the date upfront.
How should I record on my phone?
Vertical, in good light, with the phone steady and the mic close to you. We guide you before your first shoot so the edit comes out well.
What if I do not like the edit?
Tell us what feels off, with an example if you have one. We adjust it until it matches what you had in mind.
Do you add music?
Yes. For business accounts and ads we pick tracks that are safe to use, so your video is not muted or blocked.
