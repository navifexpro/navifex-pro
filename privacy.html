Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains what information Navifex Pro ("we", "us") collects when you visit navifexpro.com or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

This website does not have a contact form and does not ask you to create an account. We only receive the information you choose to send us, for example your name, phone number, email address and details about your business when you message us on WhatsApp, email us or contact us on social media.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) to keep the site secure and working well.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your messages and give you a consultation or proposal.
  • To provide the services you hire us for.
  • To keep our website secure and running properly.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We use your browser's session storage only to remember that you have already seen our loading animation.

Third-party services

When you click a WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn, Instagram or Facebook link, you leave our website and that service's own privacy policy applies.

Client data

If you become a client, we only access your social media, ad accounts or website with the permission you give us, and only for the work we agreed on. You can remove our access at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just email navifexpro@gmail.com.
