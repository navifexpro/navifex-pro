Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have. Written in plain English.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Navifex Pro ("we", "us") collects when you visit navifexpro.com or contact us, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Navifex Pro is responsible (the "controller") for this information. If you have any question, email navifexpro@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Details you send us. When you use our "Book a free call" form, website chat, the "Start your project" chat, the partner sign-up form, WhatsApp, email or social media, we receive what you choose to share: for example your name, email, WhatsApp or phone number, business or website, what you need, your preferred day, time and time zone, and any message, files, photos or voice notes.
  • Analytics data, only if you agree. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site, such as pages viewed, approximate location (country or city), device and browser type, and how you arrived.
  • Technical records. Our hosting provider keeps basic server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it, and why

  • To reply to you, arrange a call and send a proposal. We do this because you asked us to (steps before a contract) and in our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.
  • To deliver the services you hire us for, under our contract with you.
  • To pay partners their commission (partner sign-ups only), under our agreement with the partner.
  • To understand which pages help visitors, using Google Analytics, only with your consent.
  • To keep the website secure, in our legitimate interest.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decisions that affect you.

Book a free call form

When you submit the form, your details are sent over a secure (HTTPS) connection to our own system at bot.navifexpro.com, which saves them in our private Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Google Drive). Only the Navifex Pro team can see them. Your time zone is read from your device to help us suggest a suitable time; you can change it before sending. A hidden field helps us block spam.

Cookies and analytics

When you first visit, a small notice asks whether you accept analytics cookies.

  • If you click Accept, we load Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-SHX31VDL26). It sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, which usually last up to two years, and records events such as clicks on WhatsApp or phone links and form submissions.
  • If you click Decline, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

We also use your browser's local storage for a few things the site needs to work: remembering your cookie choice, whether you have already seen the intro animation in this tab, and any referral code you arrived with. These are not used to track you across other websites.

You can change your mind at any time: . You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Chat assistant

The chat on our website is an AI assistant. Your messages are sent to our AI provider only to create a reply, and we save the conversation so our team can follow up with you. Please do not share sensitive information in the chat.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run our business: Google (Workspace and, if you accept, Analytics), Cloudflare (website hosting and security), our AI provider for the chat assistant, and Meta (WhatsApp) when you message us there. They process data on our behalf or under their own privacy policies. When you click a link to WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram or Facebook, you leave our website and that service's own policy applies.

International transfers

Our team and some of our providers are based outside the UK and the European Economic Area, so your information may be processed in other countries. Where this happens, we rely on our providers' standard safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and call-request details for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client. Client records are kept for as long as we work together and for the period the law requires after that (for example, for accounting). Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

Client data

If you become a client, we only access your social media, ad accounts or website with the permission you give us, and only for the work we agreed on. You can remove our access at any time.

Partner payout details

If you join our partner program, your payout details are sent over a secure (HTTPS) connection. We keep them private and use them only to pay your commission.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under UK and EU data protection law), you can ask us to: give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or send it to you in a portable format. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw your consent to analytics at any time. Email navifexpro@gmail.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority, such as the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK.

Children

Our website and services are meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
