Questions about lead generation.

What is a lead?
A lead is someone who showed real interest, for example by sending a WhatsApp message, filling a form or calling you.
Do I need ads for this?
Not always. Tracking and better forms help even without ads. But ads are usually the fastest way to get more leads.
Will I own the data?
Yes. Your lead sheet, pixel and analytics accounts stay in your name.
Which platforms can you track?
Facebook and Instagram ads, Google Ads, your website and WhatsApp clicks, using the Meta pixel, Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager.
What do you need from me?
Access to your website, ad accounts and Facebook page, and a short chat about how your team follows up on enquiries today.
How do you report on leads?
You get a monthly summary of leads by source, plus the live sheet you can check any day.
Is this a one-time setup or monthly work?
Both are possible. Some businesses want a one-time setup, others want us to keep reviewing and improving every month. We suggest what fits in the free consultation.
