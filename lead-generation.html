FAQ

Questions about Lead Generation and Tracking.

What is a lead?

A lead is someone who showed real interest, for example by sending a message, filling in a form or calling you.

Do I need ads for this?

Not always. Tracking and better forms help even without ads. But ads are usually the fastest way to get more leads.

Will I own the data?

Yes. Your lead sheet, pixel and analytics accounts stay in your name.

Which platforms can you track?

Facebook and Instagram ads, Google Ads, your website and chat clicks, using the Meta pixel, Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager.

How do you report on leads?

You get a monthly summary of leads by source, plus the live sheet you can check any day.

Is this a one-time setup or monthly work?

Both are possible. Some businesses want a one-time setup, others want us to keep reviewing and improving every month. We suggest what fits on the free call.
