Questions about AI and WhatsApp chatbots.

Will customers know it is a bot?
Yes, and that is fine. It replies quickly and politely, and anyone who wants a person can ask for one and the chat comes to you.
Does the bot confirm payments?
No. It sends the payment screenshot to you, and you check and confirm the payment yourself.
What if it does not know an answer?
It does not guess. It tells the customer someone will reply and hands the chat to you.
Do I need a website for this?
No. You can use it only on WhatsApp or Instagram. The website chatbot is optional.
How long does setup take?
A bot on one channel is usually ready to test in about a week once we have your information. More channels or a large product list take longer.
What do you need from me?
Your products or services, delivery and return policy, timings, and the questions customers ask most. For WhatsApp, a number that can be connected to the official Meta API.
Can I change products and details later?
Yes. Tell us what changed and we update what the bot knows, so it never gives customers old information.
