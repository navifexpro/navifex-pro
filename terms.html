The exact work, timeline and price for your project are written in your proposal or invoice. Anything not listed there is outside the agreed scope and may be quoted separately.

Payments

  • Prices are in Pakistani Rupees unless your invoice says otherwise.

To do good work we need timely access to your accounts, your logo and brand details, product information and approvals. Delays in these can move the timeline.
