Terms of Service

The simple rules for working together.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

These terms apply when you use navifexpro.com or work with Navifex Pro ("we", "us"). When you hire us, you agree to these terms and to the written proposal or invoice we send you.

Our services

We provide social media management, content creation, Meta and Google Ads management, SEO, website development, video editing, AI chatbots and lead generation. Your proposal or invoice shows the exact work, timeline and price for your project. Any work not listed there is not part of the deal, and we may charge for it separately.

Payments

  • Prices are in the currency shown on your proposal or invoice.
  • Your invoice shows how you pay (in advance, in parts, or monthly). Work starts when we receive the first payment.
  • The money you spend on ads on Meta, Google or other platforms is separate from our fee. You pay it yourself.

What we need from you

To do good work, we need these from you on time: access to your accounts, your logo and brand details, product information and approvals. If these come late, the timeline can move.

Approvals and revisions

We send you designs and content to approve before they go live. Your proposal shows how many rounds of changes are included. Once you approve content, we can publish it as it is.

Ownership

Your social media pages, ad accounts, domain and website belong to you. Once a project is fully paid, the final designs, content and website files we made for you are yours to use. We may show the work in our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

Results

We work hard to grow your business and we report our results honestly. But no agency can guarantee exact sales, rankings or followers, because platforms and markets change. We never promise guaranteed results.

Monthly services and cancellation

Monthly services continue from month to month. You can stop at any time; just tell us before your next billing date. We do not refund fees already paid for work done.

Confidentiality

We keep your business information private and use it only for your project. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Partner program

Partners earn a commission only on clients who pay in full. The Earn With Us page shows the commission rates, payout timing and rules. These may change, but we will tell partners before any change.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
