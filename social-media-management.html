Questions about social media marketing.

Which platforms do you manage?
Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn. We suggest only the ones your customers actually use.
Do I need to give you photos?
Real photos of your products or place work best. If you do not have many, we can work with what you have and plan a simple shoot.
Will I see posts before they go live?
Yes. You see the calendar and the designs first, and nothing goes out without your approval.
Is there a minimum contract?
No long lock-in. You can start with a one-month trial to see how we work. After that we suggest giving social media at least three months, because pages take time to build. Terms are agreed with you before any work starts.
How do you report results?
Every month you get a short report with reach, follower growth, top posts and how many messages came in, explained in plain words with what we will change next.
Do I have to share my password?
No. You add us through Meta Business Suite as a partner. Your page stays in your name and you can remove our access at any time.
How much does it cost?
It depends on the platforms, the number of posts and whether you want reels. Tell us what you need on WhatsApp and we will send a clear quote. Any ad budget is separate.
