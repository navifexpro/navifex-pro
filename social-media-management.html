The idea

Your pages, looked after every day.

Most small businesses start a page, post for three weeks, then get busy. The page goes quiet, and when a new customer checks it before visiting, it looks like nobody's home.

That's the gap we fill. We decide what to post, design it, publish it at the right time and answer comments and DMs. Serious enquiries get passed straight to you on WhatsApp or email, so you only step in when there's money on the table.

You'll see the whole month's calendar before anything goes live, and nothing is posted without your OK.

Questions

Questions about Social Media Management.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn. We suggest only the ones your customers actually use.

Do I need to give you photos?

Real photos of your products or place work best. If you don't have many, we can work with what you have and plan a simple shoot.

Will I see posts before they go live?

Yes. You see the calendar and the designs first, and nothing goes out without your approval.

Is there a minimum contract?

No long contract. You can start with a one-month trial to see how we work. After that, we suggest at least three months, because pages take time to grow.

How do you report results?

Every month you get a short report with reach, follower growth, top posts and how many messages came in, explained in plain words with what we'll change next.

Do I have to share my password?

No. You add us through Meta Business Suite as a partner. Your page stays in your name and you can remove our access at any time.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the platforms, the number of posts and whether you want reels. Book a free call and we'll send a clear quote. Any ad budget is separate.
