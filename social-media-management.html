Your pages, handled every day.

Someone takes care of your pages every day. We decide what to post, design it, publish it on time and answer the people who comment and message.

How it works

Four steps, every time.

  1. 01

    Get to know you

    We learn about your business, your customers and the pages you already have.

  2. 02

    Plan the month

    We share a content calendar with you. You approve it or ask for changes.

  3. 03

    Design and post

    We make the posts, reels and stories, then schedule and publish them.

  4. 04

    Reply and review

    We handle comments and DMs, and at the end of the month we review and adjust.

Who it's for

Businesses that sell through their pages.

Fashion and retail brands

Real estate and education

Listing or project updates, enrollment reminders and a page that looks active when a buyer or parent checks it.

FAQ

Questions about Social Media Management.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn. We suggest only the ones your customers actually use.

Do I need to give you photos?

Real photos of your products or place work best. If you do not have many, we can work with what you have and plan a simple shoot.

Will I see posts before they go live?

Yes. You see the calendar and the designs first, and nothing goes out without your approval.

Is there a minimum contract?

No long contract. You can start with a one-month trial to see how we work. After that, we suggest at least three months, because pages take time to grow.

How do you report results?

Every month you get a short report with reach, follower growth, top posts and how many messages came in, explained in plain words with what we will change next.

Do I have to share my password?

No. You add us through Meta Business Suite as a partner. Your page stays in your name and you can remove our access at any time.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the platforms, the number of posts and whether you want reels. Book a free call and we will send a clear quote. Any ad budget is separate.
