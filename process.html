Step by step

The five steps.

  1. 01

    Discover

    In a free call we learn about your business, your customers and your goals, and look at your current pages, ads and website.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We create a simple plan: which platforms, what content, what budget, and one clear goal we will measure.

  3. 03

    Launch

    We design the content, set up the ads and tracking, or build the website, then go live.

  4. 04

    Improve

    Every week we check the numbers, test new ideas and move budget to what is working best.

  5. 05

    Report

    Every month you get an easy report: results, what we learned and the plan for next month.
