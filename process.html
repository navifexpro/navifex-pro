How we work

Five steps to steady growth.

Here's exactly how we work with every client, from the first call to the monthly report. No surprises along the way.

Step by step

The five steps.

  1. 01

    Discover

    On a free call we learn about your business, your customers and your goals, and look at your current pages, ads and website.

  2. 02

    Plan

    We write a short plan: which platforms, what content, what budget, and one clear goal we'll measure.

  3. 03

    Launch

    We design the content, set up the ads and tracking, or build the website, then go live once you've approved it.

  4. 04

    Improve

    Every week we check the numbers, try new ideas and move budget to what's working best.

  5. 05

    Report

    Every month you get a plain report: results, what we learned and the plan for next month.
