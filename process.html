Process

Five steps to steady growth.

Here is exactly how we work with every client, from the first call to the monthly report.

The five steps

01

Discover

We learn about your business, your customers and your goals in a free consultation. We also look at your current pages, ads and website.

02

Plan

We create a simple plan: which platforms, what content, what budget, and one clear goal we will measure.

03

Launch

We design the content, set up the ads and tracking, or build the website, then go live.

04

Improve

Every week we check the numbers, test new ideas and move budget to what is working best.

05

Report

Every month you get a plain-English report: results, what we learned and the plan for next month.
