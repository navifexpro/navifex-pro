FAQ

Questions about SEO.

How long does SEO take?
SEO is slow, but the results last. Small changes can help within weeks, but real growth usually takes a few months. Nobody can honestly guarantee a first-page ranking.
Do I need a new website for SEO?
Not always. Often we can improve the site you already have. If it is very slow or outdated, we will tell you.
What is a Google Business Profile?
It is your free listing on Google Maps and Search, with your address, hours, photos and reviews. For local businesses it is one of the most useful things to get right.
What do you need from me for SEO?
Access to your website and Google Business Profile, a list of your services and areas, and a little time to answer questions about your customers.
How do you report SEO progress?
Monthly, using data from Google Search Console and Analytics: the searches you appear for, clicks, calls from your Google profile and what we worked on.
Is there a minimum period for SEO?
You can start with a one-month trial to see how we work. After that we suggest at least three months, because SEO takes time to show results.
