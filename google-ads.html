The idea

Be there the moment they search.

Google Ads put your business at the top of Google when someone types in what you sell. You pay when they click.

The trick is keeping the wrong clicks out. We check the real search terms people used, block the ones that only bring job seekers or bargain hunters, and push more budget towards the searches that turn into calls and bookings.

You see every click and every cent in your own Google Ads account.

Questions

Questions about Google Ads.

Should I use Google Ads or Meta Ads?

Google is best when people search for your service. Meta is best for showing your products to new people. Many businesses use both, and we'll tell you honestly what fits.

Is the ad budget included?

No. You pay Google directly from your own account. Our work is the setup and management.

Can you target a specific city or country?

Yes. Ads can target one city, a radius around your location, several regions or several countries, and run only during the hours you choose.

What do I need to start Google Ads?

A website or landing page, a phone number or chat link, and a payment card for your Google Ads account. If you don't have a page yet, we can build one.

How do you report on Google Ads?

Each month you get a short report with spend, clicks, calls, form fills and chat clicks, plus what we changed. Everything is also visible live in your account.

Do I have to commit for a long time?

Google Ads needs a few weeks to collect data and remove wasted searches. Before we start, we agree on the first period and the terms with you.
