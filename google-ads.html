What to expect, month by month.

Google Ads needs a few weeks of data before it settles. This is the usual path.

Month 1

Setup and clean data

Keyword research, campaigns, ads and tracking for calls, forms and chat clicks. We check search terms daily at first.

Month 2

Cutting waste

We block searches that do not fit your business (negative keywords), replace weak ads and adjust bids.

Month 3

Building on what works

More budget goes to the keywords and areas that bring real enquiries. If it fits, we add display or YouTube ads for past visitors.

FAQ

Questions about Google Ads.

Should I use Google Ads or Meta Ads?
Google is best when people search for your service. Meta is best for showing your products to new people. Many businesses use both, and we will tell you honestly what fits.
Is the ad budget included?
No. You pay Google directly from your own account. Our work is the setup and management.
Can you target a specific city or country?
Yes. Ads can target one city, a radius around your location, several regions or several countries, and run only during the hours you choose.
What do I need to start Google Ads?
A website or landing page, a phone number or chat link, and a payment card for your Google Ads account. If you do not have a page yet, we can build one.
How do you report on Google Ads?
Each month you get a short report with spend, clicks, calls, form fills and chat clicks, plus what we changed. Everything is also visible live in your account.
Do I have to commit for a long time?
Google Ads needs a few weeks to collect data and remove wasted searches. Before we start, we agree on the first period and the terms with you.
