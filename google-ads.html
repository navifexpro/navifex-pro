Questions about Google Ads in Lahore.

Should I use Google Ads or Meta Ads?
Google is best when people search for your service. Meta is best for showing your products to new people. Many businesses use both, and we will tell you honestly what fits.
Is the ad budget included?
No. You pay Google directly from your own account. Our work is the setup and management.
Can you run ads for customers outside Pakistan?
Yes. We can target any country or city your business serves.
What do I need to start Google Ads?
A website or landing page, a phone or WhatsApp number, and a payment card for your Google Ads account. If you do not have a page yet, we can build one.
How do you report on Google Ads?
Each month you get a short report with spend, clicks, calls, form fills and WhatsApp clicks, plus what we changed. Everything is also visible live in your account.
Can my ads show only in Lahore?
Yes. Ads can target one city, an area around your shop or several cities, and run only during the hours you choose.
Do I have to commit for a long time?
Google Ads needs a few weeks to collect data and clean out wasted searches. We agree the starting period and terms with you before we begin.
