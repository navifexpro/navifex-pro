FAQ

Before you book.

How do we get started?

Book a free call with the form on this page, or message us on WhatsApp or email. We ask about your business and goals, then send you a short plan with clear next steps.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. We work with businesses around the world. Calls, files and reports all happen online, so distance is never a problem.

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Search, Google Business Profile and YouTube. We suggest the ones that make the most sense for your business.

Is the ad budget included in your fee?

No. The ad budget is paid directly to Meta or Google, so you always stay in control of it. Our fee covers planning, creating and managing the work.

Do I own my accounts and website?

Yes. Your social pages, ad accounts, website and data always belong to you. We only work on them with the access you give us.
