The idea

Paid reach, with one clear goal.

Meta Ads are paid posts on Facebook and Instagram, shown to the people most likely to buy from you.

We agree on one goal first: messages, leads or online sales. Then we launch a few versions, switch off the weak ones and move the budget to what's working. Every week, not every quarter.

The ad account stays in your name and the budget is paid by you straight to Meta, so you can see every dollar.

Questions

Questions about Meta Ads.

Is the ad budget included?

No. You pay the ad budget directly to Meta. Our work covers planning, setup, creatives and managing the ads.

Whose ad account is used?

Yours. The account, pages and data stay in your name, so you're always in control.

How soon will I see results?

Ads start getting views on day one, but it usually takes a few weeks of testing to find what works best. We can't promise exact numbers.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your market, product and goal. We suggest a small starting budget you're happy to test with, and raise it only when the results are good.

How will I know if the ads are working?

You get a short weekly update and a monthly report with spend, messages, leads and cost per result. You can also see everything live in your own Ads Manager.

What do you need from me?

Admin access to your Facebook page and ad account (we can help you create them), your current offers, and some real product photos or videos.
