Meta Ads for Facebook & Instagram

Ads that put your business in front of the right people on Facebook and Instagram, and bring messages, leads or sales back to you.

Paid reach, with one clear goal.

Meta Ads are paid posts on Facebook and Instagram, shown to the people most likely to buy from you.

We pick one goal, like messages, leads or online sales. Then we test, stop what is not working and move the budget to what works.

Campaigns built around one goal.

The ad budget is separate. You pay it directly to Meta from your own ad account, and you can see every dollar spent.

Campaign setup and structure

We set up your ad account, pixel and campaigns properly from day one.

Audience research and targeting

We find the people most likely to buy, by location, interests and what they do online.

Ad creatives and copy

Images, videos and text made for ads, not just reused posts.

Retargeting

We show ads again to people who visited your page or website but did not buy yet.

Message and lead campaigns

Campaigns that send people to WhatsApp, Messenger, a lead form or your inbox, ready to talk.

Weekly testing and optimization

Every week we test new audiences and creatives and move budget to what works.

How it works

Four steps, every time.

  1. 1

    Set the goal

    We agree on one goal, like messages, leads or online sales, and a budget you are comfortable with.

  2. 2

    Build the campaign

    We prepare the audiences, creatives and copy, and set up tracking.

  3. 3

    Go live

    Your ads start running. We watch them closely in the first few days.

  4. 4

    Test and improve

    Each week we pause what is weak, keep what works and try new ideas.

What to expect in the first three months.

No honest agency can promise exact numbers on day one. This is how the work usually goes.

Month 1

Setup and first tests

We set up your pixel, Business Manager and audiences properly. We launch a few ad versions and learn what people respond to.

Month 2

Cutting what does not work

We switch off weak ads and audiences, and move the budget to the ads that bring real messages and leads.

Month 3

Growing carefully

If the numbers make sense, we raise the budget step by step and add retargeting. If they do not, we tell you why.

FAQ

Questions about Meta Ads.

Is the ad budget included?
No. You pay the ad budget directly to Meta. Our work covers planning, setup, creatives and managing the ads.
Whose ad account is used?
Yours. The account, pages and data stay in your name, so you are always in control.
How soon will I see results?
Ads start getting views on day one, but it usually takes a few weeks of testing to find what works best. We cannot promise exact numbers.
How much should I spend on ads?
It depends on your market, product and goal. We suggest a small starting budget you are happy to test with, and raise it only when the results are good.
How will I know if the ads are working?
You get a short weekly update and a monthly report with spend, messages, leads and cost per result. You can also see everything live in your own Ads Manager.
What do you need from me?
Admin access to your Facebook page and ad account (we can help you create them), your current offers, and some real product photos or videos.
