01Aap ka kaam, Navifex ka kaam

Partner ke taur pe aap ka kaam sirf ek hai: aise business owners dhoondna jinhein marketing ki zaroorat hai, aur unhein Navifex tak pohanchana. Deal, kaam aur delivery Navifex ki team karti hai. Aap ko design, ads ya coding aana zaroori nahi.

Bas itna samajh lein ke har service kis masle ka hal hai. Navifex ki 9 services ye hain:

  • Social media managementInstagram, Facebook aur TikTok pages ko regular posts, captions aur replies ke saath chalana. Kis ko: shops, restaurants, salons, clinics jin ke paas time nahi.
  • Content creationPosts, carousels, reels aur product photos ke designs. Kis ko: woh brands jin ki posts purani ya kamzor lagti hain.
  • Video editingReels, YouTube videos aur ad videos ki professional editing. Kis ko: creators, coaches aur brands jo video banate hain lekin edit nahi kar paate.
  • Meta adsFacebook aur Instagram pe paid ads, sahi logon tak. Kis ko: online stores, real estate, courses, jinhein jaldi customers chahiye.
  • Google adsJab koi Google pe search kare (jaise "AC repair Lahore") to business upar nazar aaye. Kis ko: woh services jin ko log search karte hain.
  • SEOGoogle search aur Google Maps pe bina ads ke behtar ranking. Kis ko: local businesses aur websites jo search mein nazar nahi aatin.
  • Website developmentBusiness website ya Shopify online store. Ready templates bhi hain jo client dekh kar pasand kar sakta hai. Kis ko: jin ki website nahi, ya purani hai.
  • AI chatbotsWhatsApp, Instagram ya website pe 24 ghante automatic jawab. Kis ko: jo DMs ka jawab late dete hain aur customers kho dete hain.
  • Lead generation and trackingAds, forms aur tracking se aise logon ke contacts lana jo khareedna chahte hain. Kis ko: real estate, education, clinics, B2B.

Har service ke samples yahan hain: navifexpro.com/samples. Client ko kaam dikhana ho to yehi link bhejein. Website designs ke liye templates.navifexpro.com bhi dikha sakte hain.

Prices public nahi hain. Har client ki zaroorat alag hoti hai, is liye price hamesha Navifex ki team free consultation mein batati hai. Aap khud koi price ya andaza na batayein.

04Kis se baat karein

Bare brands ke peeche bhaagne se pehle un logon se shuru karein jo aap ko jaante hain, aur un businesses se jo aap ke ilaqe mein hain.

  • Jaan pehchan waleRishtedar, dost, purane classmates, jin ka business hai ya jo kisi business owner ko jaante hain. Yahan bharosa pehle se hai.
  • Local shopsClothing, mobile shops, bakery, pharmacy, hardware. Aksar Instagram page aur Google Maps listing kamzor hoti hai.
  • Restaurants aur cafesMenu ke designs, reels, Google Maps reviews aur WhatsApp ordering ka chatbot.
  • Clinics aur doctorsGoogle Maps, Google ads, appointment ke liye WhatsApp chatbot aur saaf website.
  • Real estateMeta ads aur lead generation, taake serious buyers ke contacts milein.
  • Schools aur academiesAdmissions ke waqt ads aur leads, aur social media pe regular posts.
  • Salons, gyms aur online brandsContent, reels, Instagram management, Shopify store aur ads.

Kaise pehchanein ke zaroorat hai

Message se pehle 2 minute business ko check karein. Jo kami nazar aaye, wohi aap ki baat ka point hai.

  • Website nahi hai→Website ya ready template
  • Instagram pe 2 se 3 mahine se koi post nahi→Social media management
  • Designs purane ya dhundle→Content creation
  • Google Maps pe nahi milta, ya reviews kam→SEO aur Maps listing
  • DM ka jawab ghanton baad→AI chatbot
  • Videos hain lekin editing kachi→Video editing
  • Ads chal rahe hain lekin results nahi→Meta ads, Google ads, lead tracking

Ek list banayein: business ka naam, number ya Instagram, kya kami dekhi, kab message kiya, kya jawab aaya. Roz 10 naye naam.

07Client aap ke zariye kyun aaye

Client seedha bhi Navifex ko message kar sakta hai. Phir aap ke zariye kyun aaye? Sirf do wajah hain, aur yehi sach sach batayein:

  • 1. Aap ki personal madadAap consultation set karte hain, client ki baat team tak saaf pohanchate hain, follow-up karte hain, aur koi masla ho to client aap ko bata sakta hai. Aik jaana pehchana banda saath hota hai.
  • 2. Aap ke code se 5% discountAap ka code (NVX-XXXX) ya link use karne pe client ko 5% off milta hai.

Is ke ilawa kuch offer nahi hota. Ye cheezein na kahein:

  • Apni taraf se extra discount5% ke ilawa koi discount nahi. Apni commission mein se "kuch wapas" dene ka waada bhi na karein.
  • Free kaam ya bonus"Ek post free", "logo free" jaisi cheezein aap ke haath mein nahi.
  • Price ya resultsPrice team batati hai. Sales, followers ya ranking ki guarantee koi nahi deta.
Client ko ye kaise batayein
Aap direct bhi Navifex se baat kar sakte hain. Mere zariye aane ka faida ye hai ke main poore process mein aap ke saath rahunga: consultation set karna, follow up karna, aur agar koi baat ho to aap mujhe bata sakte hain. Aur mere code [NVX-XXXX] se aap ko 5% off milta hai.

Rules

  • Spam nahiBulk messages, groups mein baar baar link, ya ek banday ko 3 se zyada messages nahi.
  • Jhoote waade nahiResults, sales ya followers ki guarantee nahi. Khud se price ya discount nahi. Discount sirf code wala 5%.
  • SachKhud ko partner kahein, employee nahi. Navifex ka logo apni profile photo na banayein.
  • Izzat"Na" ka matlab na hai. Raat der se message ya call nahi.
  • PrivacyClient ki baat, number ya details kisi aur ko ya public mein share na karein.
  • CreditNavifex ke samples share karein to credit dein. Kisi ka kaam apna na kahein.

Weekly routine (roz takreeban 1 ghanta)

Monday10 naye businesses list mein, Lesson 4 se check, 10 personal messages.
Tuesday1 post + stories. Purane messages pe follow-up.
Wednesday10 naye messages. 3 logon se references maangein.
Thursday1 post. 2 dukanon pe ja kar mulaqat, ya 3 calls.
Friday10 naye messages. Follow-ups. Interested logon ko Navifex tak pohanchayein.
Saturday1 post. Hafte ka hisaab: kitne messages, kitne jawab, kitne consultations, kya behtar karna hai.

Lead sheet ke columns

Date · Business ka naam · Type (shop, clinic...) · Number / Instagram · Kya kami dekhi · Kaise mila (khud, reference) · Pehla message · Follow-up dates · Status (New, Talking, Sent to Navifex, Paid, No) · Notes

Har koi jawab nahi deta, aur ye normal hai. Roz thora kaam, achi reputation aur sachi baat, yehi asal tareeqa hai.
