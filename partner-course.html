Lessons

7 lessons, step by step.

01Navifex kya bechta hai aur kis ko

Aap ko technical kaam nahi aana chahiye. Bas itna samjhein ke har service kis masle ka hal hai. Kaam Navifex ki team karti hai.

  • Social media managementBusiness ke Instagram, Facebook, TikTok pages ko regular posts, captions aur replies ke saath chalana. Kis ko: shops, restaurants, salons, clinics jin ke paas time nahi.
  • Content creationAchay posts, carousels, reels aur product photos ke designs. Kis ko: har woh brand jis ki posts purani ya bekaar lagti hain.
  • Video editingReels, YouTube videos aur ad videos ko professional banana. Kis ko: creators, coaches, brands jo videos to banate hain lekin edit nahi kar paate.
  • Meta adsFacebook aur Instagram pe paid ads, sahi logon tak. Kis ko: online stores, real estate, courses, jo jaldi customers chahte hain.
  • Google adsJab koi Google pe search kare (jaise "AC repair Lahore") to business upar nazar aaye. Kis ko: services jin ko log search karte hain.
  • SEOGoogle pe bina ads ke (free) ranking, aur Google Maps listing behtar karna. Kis ko: local businesses aur websites jo search mein nazar nahi aatin.
  • Websites aur Shopify storesBusiness website ya online store. Navifex ke paas 150 ready templates bhi hain jo client dekh kar pasand kar sakta hai. Kis ko: jin ki website hi nahi, ya purani hai.
  • AI chatbotsWhatsApp, Instagram ya website pe 24/7 automatic replies. Kis ko: jo DMs ka jawab late dete hain aur customers kho dete hain.
  • Lead generationAds, forms aur tracking se aise logon ke contacts lana jo khareedna chahte hain. Kis ko: real estate, education, clinics, B2B.

Prices public nahi hain. Har client ki zaroorat alag hoti hai, is liye price hamesha Navifex team batati hai. Aap khud koi price na batayein.

02Pehle 10 clients kahan milenge

Bare clients ke peeche mat bhaagein. Shuru wahan se karein jahan log aap ko pehle se jaante hain.

  • 1. Apna circleRishtedar, dost, purane classmates, jin ka koi business hai ya jo kisi business owner ko jaante hain. Yahan trust pehle se hai.
  • 2. Mohalle ki local shopsSalon, bakery, clothing shop, pharmacy, gym, tuition academy. Khud ja kar baat karna sab se asar wala tareeqa hai.
  • 3. Instagram businessesHome bakers, clothing brands, jewellery, makeup artists. Inhein website, behtar content ya chatbot ki zaroorat hoti hai.
  • 4. Facebook groupsBusiness owners ke groups mein log aksar poochte hain "koi achi marketing agency batayein". Wahan madadgar jawab dein, spam nahi.
  • 5. Google Maps listingsMaps pe "restaurant near me" ya "clinic" search karein. Jin ki listing mein website ka button nahi, woh aap ke leads hain.
  • 6. OLX aur Daraz sellersJo sellers regular bechte hain, unhein apna store (Shopify) ya Instagram page grow karne mein dilchaspi ho sakti hai.

Ek simple list banayein (notebook ya Google Sheet): business ka naam, number/Instagram, kya kami dekhi, message kab bheja, jawab kya aaya.

03Kaise pehchanein ke business ko zaroorat hai

Message bhejne se pehle 2 minute business ko check karein. Jo kami nazar aaye, wohi aap ki baat ka point hai.

  • Website nahi haiWebsite ya ready template
  • Instagram kamzor hai (kam followers, ghatiya designs)Social media management + content
  • Google Business profile nahi (Maps pe nahi milta)SEO aur Maps listing
  • DMs ka jawab nahi aata ya bohat lateAI chatbot
  • Posts purani hain (2-3 mahine se kuch nahi)Social media management
  • Videos hain lekin edit kachi haiVideo editing
  • Ads chal rahe, lekin results nahiMeta ads, Google ads, lead tracking

Business ki burai na karein. "Aap ka page bekaar hai" ke bajaye kahein "Aap ka product acha hai, bas posts thori purani hain."

05Objections ka jawab

"Na" ya shak hona normal hai. Behas na karein, araam se jawab dein.

1. "Bohat mehnga hoga."
"Price aap ki zaroorat pe depend karti hai. Free consultation mein team aap ke budget ke hisaab se plan batati hai, aur mere code se 5% off bhi milega."
2. "Hum khud post kar lete hain."
"Ye to achi baat hai. Team sirf woh kaam sambhal sakti hai jis ka aap ke paas time nahi, jaise designs, reels ya ads. Ek dafa consultation mein dekh lein."
3. "Pehle bhi agency try ki, faida nahi hua."
"Samajh sakta hoon. Isi liye pehle free consultation hai. Woh honestly batayenge kya kaam karega aur kya nahi, phir aap faisla karein."
4. "Abhi time nahi, baad mein."
"Koi masla nahi. Main agle hafte yaad dila doon? Tab tak aap un ka kaam dekh lein: navifexpro.com/work"
5. "Aap kaun ho, trust kaise karein?"
"Main Navifex Pro ka partner hoon. Aap direct agency se WhatsApp +92 313 0111349 pe baat karein ge aur payment bhi direct agency ko hogi. Website: navifexpro.com"

Kabhi results ki guarantee na dein (jaise "pakki sales" ya "itne followers"). Sirf ye kahein ke team free consultation mein honest plan batayegi.

07Rules aur weekly routine

  • Spam nahiBulk messages, groups mein baar baar link, ya ek hi banday ko kai messages nahi. Spam se Navifex aur aap dono ka naam kharab hota hai.
  • Jhoote waade nahiResults, sales ya followers ki guarantee na dein, aur khud se price ya discount na batayein. Discount sirf code wala 5% hai.
  • Izzat aur sach"Na" ka matlab na hai. Raat der se message na karein. Khud ko Navifex ka employee nahi, partner batayein.

Ek simple routine (rozana lagbhag 1 ghanta):

Mon to FriRoz 10 businesses dhoondein, Lesson 3 ki checklist se check karein, aur personal message bhejein.
Har roz3-4 din purane messages pe ek follow-up. Jawab aur notes apni list mein likhein.
SaturdayInterested logon ko Navifex tak pohanchayein (link, WhatsApp ya group intro).
SundayHafte ka hisaab: kitne messages, kitne jawab, kya behtar ho sakta hai.

Har koi jawab nahi deta, aur ye bilkul normal hai. Earnings sirf un clients se hoti hain jo asal mein pay karte hain, is liye mustaqil mizaji aur achi reputation sab se kaam aati hai.
