FAQ

Questions we often get.

How do we get started?
Send us a message on WhatsApp or email. We will book a free consultation, learn about your business and goals, and then share a simple plan with clear next steps.
Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?
Yes. We work with businesses in Pakistan and around the world. Everything is done online — calls, shared files and reports — so your location is not a problem.
Which platforms do you manage?
Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Search, Google Business Profile and YouTube. We will suggest the ones that make the most sense for your business.
How soon will I see results?
Paid ads can bring enquiries within the first few weeks. SEO and organic social media take longer, usually two to three months, to build steady growth. We will set honest expectations at the start.
Is the ad budget included in your fee?
No. The ad budget is paid directly to Meta or Google, so you always stay in control of it. Our fee is for planning, creating and managing the work.
Do I own my accounts and website?
Yes. Your social pages, ad accounts, website and data always belong to you. We only work on them with the access you give us.
Can you build a website for my business?
Yes. We build business websites, landing pages and online stores that load fast, work well on mobile and are ready for ads and SEO.
