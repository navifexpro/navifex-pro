FAQ

Quick answers.

What does it cost?

It depends on what your business needs: which services, how many posts or pages, and your ad goals. Book a free call and we will send you a clear quote, usually within a day. The call itself is free.

Do you work with businesses in my country?

Yes. We work with businesses around the world. Calls, files and reports all happen online, and we schedule calls in your time zone.

How soon will I see results?

Ads can bring enquiries in the first few weeks. SEO and organic social media take longer. Steady growth usually takes two to three months, and we give you honest timelines from day one.

Do I own my accounts and website?

Yes. Your social pages, ad accounts, website and data always belong to you. We only work on them with the access you give us.

Is the ad budget included in your fee?

No. The ad budget is paid directly to Meta or Google, so you always stay in control of it. Our fee covers planning, creating and managing the work.
