FAQ

Questions we often get.

What does it cost?
It depends on what your business needs: which services, how many posts or pages, and your ad goals. Send us a WhatsApp message with a little about your business and we will reply with a clear quote, usually the same day. The first consultation is free.
Is my business too small for an agency?
No. A lot of our work is with small shops, home businesses and new brands. We suggest a small start that fits your budget, and we add more only when it is working for you.
How do we get started?
Message us on WhatsApp or email. We will set up a free call, ask about your business and goals, and then send you a short plan with clear next steps.
Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?
Yes. We work with businesses in Pakistan and abroad. Calls, files and reports all happen online, so distance is never a problem.
Which platforms do you manage?
Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Search, Google Business Profile and YouTube. We will suggest the ones that make the most sense for your business.
How soon will I see results?
Ads can bring enquiries in the first few weeks. SEO and free social media posts take longer. Steady growth usually takes two to three months. We give you honest timelines from day one.
Is the ad budget included in your fee?
No. The ad budget is paid directly to Meta or Google, so you always stay in control of it. Our fee is for planning, creating and managing the work.
Do I own my accounts and website?
Yes. Your social pages, ad accounts, website and data always belong to you. We only work on them with the access you give us.
Can you build a website for my business?
Yes. We build business websites, landing pages and online stores that load fast, work well on mobile and are ready for ads and SEO.
