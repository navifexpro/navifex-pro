FAQ

Questions about websites.

How long does a website take?
A landing page or small business site usually takes a few days. Bigger sites and stores take a little longer.
Can I ask for changes?
Yes. Revisions are included, and we keep working until the site feels right to you.
Will the website be mine?
Yes. The domain and website belong to you, and we hand over all the logins.
What do you need from me to start?
Your logo, some text about your business (or notes we can write from), photos, and a few websites you like. If you do not have a domain yet, we help you choose one.
Do you provide hosting?
We can set up hosting for you or use the one you already have. Either way, the account stays in your name.
Can I update the website myself?
Yes. We build on platforms you can edit, and show you how to change text, photos and products.
Will my website work well on mobile?
Yes. Most visitors in Pakistan come from phones, so we design for mobile first and test on real devices before launch.
