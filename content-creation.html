Questions about content creation.

What do you need from us?
Your logo, a few product or shop pictures and the answers to our business form. For reels, some phone clips. We guide you on what to record.
Can the content be in Urdu?
Yes. English, Urdu, Roman Urdu or a mix, whatever your customers prefer.
Will you post it too?
If you also take our social media management, yes, we schedule and post everything and reply to comments.
Can we change something before it goes live?
Always. Nothing is posted until you have seen it.
How many posts do I get each month?
That depends on your goals and budget. We agree the number of posts, carousels and reels before we start, and it stays the same each month unless you ask to change it.
How soon is the first content ready?
Once you fill our business form, the first monthly plan usually comes within a few days. Designs follow as soon as you approve the plan.
Who owns the designs?
You do. All final designs and videos are yours to use on any platform, including ads.
