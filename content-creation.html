How it works

Four steps, every time.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your business

    A short form: what you sell, who buys it, your offers, your style and the content you like.

  2. 2

    We plan the month

    You get a content plan with post ideas, hooks and dates. You approve it or ask for changes.

  3. 3

    We create

    We design the posts, write the captions and edit the reels. We tell you exactly what to film.

  4. 4

    Review and post

    You check everything before it goes out. We can post it for you as well.

FAQ

Questions about Content Creation.

What do you need from us?
Your logo, a few product or location photos and the answers to our short business form. For reels, some phone clips. We guide you on what to record.
Will you post it too?
Yes, if you also take our social media management. Then we schedule and post everything and reply to comments.
Can we change something before it goes live?
Always. Nothing is posted until you have seen it.
How many posts do I get each month?
That depends on your goals and budget. We agree the number of posts, carousels and reels before we start, and it stays the same each month unless you ask to change it.
How soon is the first content ready?
Once you fill in our business form, the first monthly plan usually comes within a few days. Designs follow as soon as you approve the plan.
Who owns the designs?
You do. All final designs and videos are yours to use on any platform, including ads.
